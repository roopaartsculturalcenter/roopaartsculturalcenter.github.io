Our Mission

Promote. Produce. Provide access.

Our mission is to promote, produce, and provide access to a diverse range of arts experiences — encompassing the visual, media, and performing arts. As a nonprofit, we rely on the generosity of our supporters to keep programs running and accessible to all.

Signature Festival

The Arudra Festival

Our flagship annual celebration of South Indian classical arts, bringing dancers, musicians, and the community together on one stage. Arudra 2026 featured Trilokya Nada, a Veena-Violin duet, Navagrahamum Navakailasamum, and Thiruvadhirai Threads at the Jewish Community Center of Houston.
